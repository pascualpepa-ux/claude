DESCRIPCIÓN DEL CANAL (pegar en YouTube Studio, Personalización, Información básica)

Rumbo Traveling is a free website that helps you plan your trips in minutes. Answer a few questions and it builds a day-by-day itinerary with the real weather, along with flights, hotels and the budget per person. It also keeps track of your group's expenses and tells you who owes whom, and it has travel guides with prices for cities like Rome, Paris and Tokyo. Plan your trip for free at https://rumbotraveling.com/?lang=en
